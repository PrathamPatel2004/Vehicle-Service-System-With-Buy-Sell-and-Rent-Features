About Us

Vehicle Service Center Your Trusted Vehicle Care Experts

At Vehicle Service Center, with a passion for all things automotive, our mission is to provide the best service possible at a fair price and in a timely manner.

  • Work by a team of skilled and passionate Mechanics.
  • Transportation to and from your location.
  • Providing Emergency Services whether it's a breakdown or a flat tire at any time of the day or night.

We believe in honesty, transparency, and good old-fashioned customer care. That’s why so many of our clients keep coming back — and why they recommend us to their friends and family.

Drive in anytime. Drive out with peace of mind.

Services

Full Vehicle Diagnostic

Comprehensive diagnostic scanning to detect issues with engine, brakes, transmission, and more—ensuring optimal performance.

Routine Maintenance Service

Scheduled oil changes, fluid top-ups, filter replacements, and system checks to keep your car running smoothly and safely.

Emergency Onsite Services

Stuck on the road? We offer mobile jump-starts, fuel delivery, battery replacement, and minor mechanical fixes at your location.

Car Wash & Vacuuming

Exterior hand wash and full interior vacuuming to restore that fresh, clean look—because your car deserves a spa day too.

Paint Protection & Polishing

Protect your vehicle’s finish with premium waxing, ceramic coating, and machine polishing for a long-lasting shine.

Tyre Replacement & Oil Change

Quick and professional tyre replacement, rotation, and high-grade oil changes to keep your engine and wheels in top shape.
